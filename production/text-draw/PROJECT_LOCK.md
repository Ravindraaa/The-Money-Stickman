# Project Lock — The Money Stickman

**Status:** APPROVED / CREATIVE BASELINE LOCKED  
**Approved branch:** `text-draw-motion-pilot`  
**Baseline commit:** `3252e8c48bef865d1a13cfc146b84489b6014c53`  
**Baseline episode:** “Why Your First $1,000 Is So Hard to Save”

This file records the approved production standard. Treat the baseline as frozen. Do not redesign, refactor, replace, or “improve” approved elements unless the owner explicitly requests it.

## Locked creative standard

- Keep the approved visual output and overall visual identity exactly as the reference.
- Keep the approved narration voice, voice quality, delivery, and processing unchanged.
- Keep the established 9:16 vertical format and 1080×1920 output.
- Preserve the approved typography, font hierarchy, colours, backgrounds, layout, spacing, composition, and visual density.
- Preserve the approved motion language: animation character, speed, easing, transitions, layering, depth, and micro-movements.
- Preserve scene pacing, narration synchronization, timing logic, audio treatment, and final audio/video alignment.
- Keep the established text/draw-based finance motion-graphics approach. Do not introduce stickmen, people, characters, or unrelated visual styles.
- Keep visual storytelling synchronized to the exact narration line. Show the relevant object, number, chart, flow, or action while it is being discussed.
- Maintain existing QA and render checks. Intentional overlaps must be explicitly marked for visual QA rather than removed if they are part of the approved design.

## Allowed changes for future episodes

Only episode-specific content may change:
- Script, subject, facts, examples, and call to action.
- Scene-specific illustrations, labels, numbers, charts, and finance objects needed to tell that episode’s story.
- Scene durations only when required by the new narration; preserve the same pacing feel and synchronization standard.

## Changes requiring explicit approval

Do not change any of the following without asking first:
- Narration voice, TTS engine, voice settings, or audio processing.
- Global design system, typography, colours, layout, backgrounds, or visual identity.
- Animation system, transitions, motion timing/easing, or scene architecture.
- Aspect ratio, resolution, render pipeline, timeline synchronization, or QA thresholds.
- Dependencies, workflow structure, build configuration, or production scripts.

## Production rule

For every new episode, duplicate the approved baseline and change only episode-specific content. Render, inspect the complete video (including the ending), verify narration is present and synchronized, and confirm the final artifact before calling it complete.

**Golden rule:** New story, same approved output. No silent redesigns or unrelated changes.
