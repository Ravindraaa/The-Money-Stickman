# Short 01 — Your Paycheck Disappears Fast

**Audience:** United States  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical  
**Target runtime:** 35–40 seconds  
**Style:** Existing 2D Money Stickman character; energetic but clear American male narration  
**Status:** Script and timing prepared; voice audition pending

## Hook (0:00–0:06)
**VO:** "Your paycheck hits… and somehow, it’s already gone. Where did it all go?"  
**On-screen:** YOUR PAYCHECK DISAPPEARS FAST.  
**Action:** Stickman celebrates payday; money flies in, then starts disappearing.

## The math (0:06–0:19)
**VO:** "Let’s say you bring home two thousand dollars. Rent and utilities take a thousand and fifty. Food and transportation? Four-twenty. Debt payments and subscriptions? Three-ten."  
**On-screen:**  
- Take-home pay: $2,000  
- Rent + utilities: −$1,050  
- Food + transportation: −$420  
- Debt + subscriptions: −$310  
**Action:** Each expense slams onto the screen; the balance counts down.

## The reveal (0:19–0:26)
**VO:** "That leaves just two hundred and twenty dollars. And that’s before the random stuff life throws at you."  
**On-screen:** ONLY $220 LEFT  
**Action:** Stickman checks empty wallet; surprise bills pop in.

## One practical move (0:26–0:37)
**VO:** "Try this on your next payday: automatically move even twenty dollars into savings first. Start small, make it a habit, then build from there."  
**On-screen:** PAY YOURSELF FIRST. START WITH $20.  
**Action:** $20 moves into a savings jar; stickman celebrates.

## CTA (0:37–0:40)
**VO:** "Follow The Money Stickman for money moves that actually make sense."  
**On-screen:** FOLLOW FOR SIMPLE MONEY MOVES.

## Accuracy note
The $2,000 take-home pay and expense amounts are illustrative numbers for the story, not national averages or financial advice. The $20 savings suggestion is a beginner habit, not a guaranteed fit for every budget.

## Voice audition line
"Your paycheck just hit. Nice! But wait… where did all that money go? Let’s break it down."
