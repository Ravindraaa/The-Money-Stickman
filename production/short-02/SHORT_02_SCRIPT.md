# Short 02 — The 24-Hour Rule That Stops Impulse Buying

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 35 seconds
**Voice:** Approved Voice B style; energetic conversational American English

## 0:00–0:07 — Hook
"Stop! Before you buy that thing online… try this. Your brain wants it NOW. That's exactly how impulse spending wins."

## 0:07–0:23 — The rule
"Add it to your cart… then wait 24 hours. Tomorrow, ask yourself three questions: Do I need it? Can I afford it? Will I still want it?"

## 0:23–0:35 — Payoff + CTA
"If the answer is no, close that tab. You just avoided an unplanned purchase. Follow The Money Stickman for simple money rules that actually stick."

## Accuracy note
The 24-hour pause is a practical behavioral tip, not a guarantee of savings or a substitute for a personal budget.
