# Short 06 — Invest $100 a Month the Simple Way

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 45 seconds
**Voice:** upbeat, quick, conversational American English; minimal pauses

## Narration

Want to invest but feel lost? Don't pick stocks. Do this instead. Buy an index fund. One fund, hundreds of companies. Big names you know, all in one. One bad company won't sink the fund. Low fees keep more money yours. Fees quietly eat returns for decades. Pick a low-cost one. Check the expense ratio. Now the secret: consistency. Invest the same amount every month. Market up? Keep going. Market down? Keep going. That's called dollar-cost averaging. It removes the stress of timing. Even experts can't time the market. Time in the market beats timing it. Start early, even small. Compounding does the heavy lifting. Start in your 401(k) or a Roth IRA. Not a guarantee. Investments can lose value. Follow for one money lesson a day.
