# Short 03 — The $5 Habit That Costs $1,825 a Year

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 35 seconds
**Voice:** Approved Voice B style; energetic, conversational American English

## 0:00–0:08 — Hook
"That five-dollar purchase feels harmless, right? A coffee on the way to work, a snack between errands, or one little convenience you barely think about."

## 0:08–0:23 — The math
"But when that same five-dollar purchase happens every single day, the total gets surprisingly big. You are spending eighteen hundred and twenty-five dollars in one year. Here is the math. Five dollars, multiplied by three hundred and sixty-five days, equals eighteen hundred and twenty-five dollars. And that is the point. It is not one huge purchase that drains your wallet. It is a tiny habit, repeated so often that you stop noticing it."

## 0:23–0:35 — Payoff + CTA
"You do not have to cut every little joy, and you do not need to feel guilty about buying coffee. Just choose one daily expense you barely notice. Ask yourself: is this small habit really worth eighteen hundred dollars a year? If it is, enjoy it. If not, make a different choice and keep that money for something you actually want. Small spends add up. Follow The Money Stickman for money moves that make sense."

## Accuracy note
$5 × 365 = $1,825. This is an illustrative annualized example assuming the $5 purchase happens every day; it is not a claim about typical consumer spending.
