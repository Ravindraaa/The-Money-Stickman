# Short 03 — The $5 Habit That Costs $1,825 a Year

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 35 seconds
**Voice:** Approved Voice B style; energetic, conversational American English

## 0:00–0:08 — Hook
"That five-dollar purchase feels harmless, right? A coffee, a snack, one little convenience."

## 0:08–0:23 — The math
"But do it every single day, and you're spending eighteen hundred and twenty-five dollars a year. Here's the math: five dollars times three hundred sixty-five days. That's not a random big purchase. It's a tiny habit on repeat."

## 0:23–0:35 — Payoff + CTA
"You don't have to cut every little joy. Just pick one daily expense you barely notice, and decide if it's worth eighteen hundred dollars a year. Small spends add up. Follow The Money Stickman for money moves that make sense."

## Accuracy note
$5 × 365 = $1,825. This is an illustrative annualized example assuming the $5 purchase happens every day; it is not a claim about typical consumer spending.
