# Short 04 — The Credit Card Minimum Payment Trap

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 35 seconds  
**Voice:** Approved Voice B; energetic, conversational American English

## 0:00–0:08 — Hook
"Only paying the minimum on your credit card? That small payment can keep you in debt longer than you expect."

## 0:08–0:23 — The interest trap
"Here's why. Your minimum payment covers only part of what you owe. The rest of your balance can keep collecting interest month after month. So even after making a payment, your debt may shrink much more slowly than you think."

## 0:23–0:35 — Action + CTA
"Check your balance, know your interest rate, and pay more than the minimum when you can. And try not to add new charges while paying it down. Your credit card should work for you, not against you. Follow The Money Stickman for money moves that make sense."

## Accuracy note
Credit-card minimum-payment formulas and interest terms vary by issuer and account. This short explains the general risk without claiming a specific payoff period or interest amount.
