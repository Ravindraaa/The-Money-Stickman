# Short 04 — The Credit Card Minimum Payment Trap

**Audience:** United States | **Format:** 9:16 | **Target:** 35 seconds  
**Voice:** Approved Voice B; conversational American English

## 0:00–0:08 — Hook
"Only paying the minimum on your credit card? That small payment can keep you in debt much longer than you expect."

## 0:08–0:23 — The interest trap
"Here's the trap. Your minimum payment covers only part of what you owe. The unpaid balance can keep collecting interest, month after month. So even when you make every payment on time, your debt may shrink much more slowly than you think. And if you keep adding new purchases, you can make it even harder to get ahead."

## 0:23–0:35 — Action + CTA
"Start by checking your full balance and understanding your interest rate. Then pay more than the minimum whenever you can, even if it's a little extra. Avoid new charges while you're paying it down. Your credit card should work for you, not against you. Follow The Money Stickman for money moves that make sense."

## Accuracy note
Credit-card minimum-payment formulas and interest terms vary by issuer and account. This short explains the general risk without claiming a specific payoff period or interest amount.
